FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop and tap Restore (top right), or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Does the Aura Pass renew automatically?

No. The Aura Pass lasts 4 weeks and then switches off by itself. You are never charged again unless you buy it again.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too. Purchases can always be restored.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Videos are optional and only play when you tap Watch to get a bonus.

How do I change ad or tracking choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad consent (EU/UK): in the game's Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
