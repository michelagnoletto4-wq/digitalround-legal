Gatekept – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Gatekept ("the app") is made by Michele Agnoletto ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device. The app does not require an account and we do not run servers that store your progress.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them on reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history. This data is not linked to your name or contact details. RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one to get a bonus). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), an approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and how you interact with the app, and crash and performance data. It uses this data to show and measure ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised and the game works the same. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use any other analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

You can delete all game data by deleting the app, reset tracking permission in iOS Settings, and change ad consent in the app's settings. For any privacy question, contact us.
